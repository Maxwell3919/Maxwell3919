## Research focus · 研究方向

- **Electronic structure and DFT:** physical models, convergence, provenance, and claim–evidence boundaries.
- **Two-dimensional materials:** superconductivity, van der Waals heterostructures, DFPT, and electron–phonon coupling.
- **Scientific software and AI:** reliable agent workflows, deterministic validation, and research knowledge infrastructure.
